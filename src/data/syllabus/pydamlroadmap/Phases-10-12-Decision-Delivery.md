# Phases 10–12 — Experiments, BI & Professional Delivery

## Phase 10 · Days 109–120 · Inference & Experiment Design

Confidence intervals; hypothesis logic; tests for means, proportions, and categories; resampling; effect size; power and sample size; multiple comparisons; experiment units and randomization; A/B metrics and guardrails; decision memos; **Museum Wayfinding Experiment**.

The gate requires pre-specified outcomes, power assumptions, accessibility guardrails, intent-to-treat reasoning, uncertainty, and an expand/revise/measure-more decision.

## Phase 11 · Days 121–132 · Tableau, BI, Time Series & Metric Systems

Tableau connections and mental model; visual encoding; filter order; calculated fields and LODs; table calculations; dashboards and actions; accessibility/performance/publishing; descriptive time-series structure; rolling and seasonal baselines; assumption-led scenarios; metric trees and data contracts; **Emergency Shelter Operations Command Centre**.

The gate requires governed definitions, quality status, an accessible dashboard, refresh ownership, seasonal context, explicit scenario assumptions, a resource memo, and incident fallback.

## Phase 12 · Days 133–144 · Professional Analytics, Communication & Portfolio

Decision contracts; analytical plans; stakeholder interviews; quality incidents; executive writing; data storytelling; ethics and privacy; portfolio case studies; Python/Pandas interviews; SQL/Excel/BI interviews; statistics/product cases; **Watershed Restoration Funding Portfolio**.

The gate requires transparent criteria, Python/SQL analysis, an Excel reviewer model, a Tableau decision view, sensitivity and fairness review, an executive presentation, technical defense, inspectable case study, and changed-budget transfer.
