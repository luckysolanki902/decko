# Python & Data Analytics — 144-Day Syllabus

This is a deep analyst curriculum: 12 phases, 144 continuous days, about 588 guided hours, delayed review, and a distinct decision-facing capstone in every phase. The structured source of truth is `src/data/phases/daml/index.ts`; the documents here mirror it for human review.

## Boundary

Included: Python, useful OOP, files and errors, NumPy, Pandas, Excel, SQL, Tableau/BI, EDA from Phase 1, visualization, descriptive and inferential statistics, probability, sampling, experiments and A/B tests, non-predictive time-series analysis, metric design, quality, communication, portfolio work, and analyst interviews.

Predictive-model development, content-generation systems, browser or desktop control, data-harvesting tracks, and general-purpose automation are outside this analyst curriculum.

## Learning and evidence contract

No programming or statistics background is assumed. Day 1 diagnoses setup, table-reading, and percentage gaps and supplies a repair route. Every day uses failure before rule, a mental model, prediction, a worked case, independent reconstruction, and a changed task. Review begins around 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days and adapts to evidence. Phase gates require a reproducible artifact, diagnosed failure, disclosed help, delayed reconstruction, and transfer; completion alone is not mastery.

Every project names its user and decision, protects raw inputs, documents grain and assumptions, checks correctness, states uncertainty and non-goals, and separates observation from interpretation and recommendation.
