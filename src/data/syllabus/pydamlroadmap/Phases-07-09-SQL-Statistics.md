# Phases 7–9 — SQL, Data Models & Statistical Foundations

## Phase 7 · Days 73–84 · SQL Query Foundations

Relational grain; filtering and three-valued logic; sorting, distinctness and CASE; aggregation; joins; multi-table paths; subqueries and CTEs; dates/text; set operations; quality queries; explain basics; **Repair Café Parts & Visit Analysis**.

The gate requires a relationship map, cardinality checks, source reconciliation, metric definitions, a staffing-and-parts brief, and roster-change transfer.

## Phase 8 · Days 85–96 · Advanced SQL & Analytical Data Modeling

Window functions; rolling measures; lag and lead; cohorts; funnels; recursion; facts and dimensions; slowly changing dimensions; metric contracts; indexes and performance; safe transactional writes; **Community Bicycle Share Reliability Mart**.

The gate requires a dimensional grain contract, tested metric SQL, performance evidence, safe loads, a crew-deployment brief, and transfer to a second municipality.

## Phase 9 · Days 97–108 · Descriptive Statistics, Probability & Sampling

Centre/spread/shape; percentiles and robust comparisons; correlation and confounding; probability; Bayes with natural frequencies; random variables and expectation; common distributions; populations and selection; sampling designs; sampling distributions and standard error; survey measurement and weighting; **Rural Broadband Experience Survey**.

The gate requires a defensible frame and design, measurement review, weighted/unweighted comparison, sensitivity analysis, representativeness limits, and bounded advocacy claims.
