# Phase 5 — Device Capabilities & Native Product Features

**Days 23–28 · Outcome:** use device capabilities through recoverable, privacy-respecting permission flows.

- Day 23: permission states, just-in-time rationale, app config, native rebuild triggers, settings recovery, and minimization.
- Day 24: camera, image picker, limited-library access, files, compression, metadata, upload progress, cancellation, retry, and cleanup.
- Day 25: location accuracy, last-known/current positions, maps, regions, markers, clustering, battery, privacy, and spoofing awareness.
- Day 26: local/push notifications, tokens, channels, actions, foreground/background/killed receipt, deep links, and duplicate handling.
- Day 27: sensors, subscriptions, sampling, noise, haptics, background constraints, battery budgets, and graceful degradation.
- Day 28: build Neighborhood Tree Walk for families choosing nearby stops and recording private observations. Combine map stops, optional foreground location, optional leaf photos, a clearly labelled local/mock upload queue, reminders, heading feedback, and semantic haptics. Prove denied, approximate, interrupted, offline, cancelled, and low-battery paths; then add an indoor trail with no coordinates. Species identification, public reporting, background tracking, and turn-by-turn directions stay out of scope.
