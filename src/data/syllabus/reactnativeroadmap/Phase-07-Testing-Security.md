# Phase 7 — Testing, Debugging & Mobile Security

**Days 35–39 · Outcome:** prove critical behavior across platforms and protect user data within the limits of an untrusted client device.

- Day 35: Jest, deterministic logic, timers, React Native Testing Library, user events, accessibility queries, and behavior-first assertions.
- Day 36: integrated navigation/Redux/network/storage tests, mock servers, Maestro/Detox trade-offs, permissions, deep links, and flake control.
- Day 37: DevTools, Metro, source maps, network, logcat, iOS console, symbols, release-only failures, caches, rebuilds, and `expo-doctor`.
- Day 38: threat models, SecureStore, tokens, biometrics, screenshots, clipboard, TLS, deep-link validation, WebViews, PII, and redaction.
- Day 39: build the Youth Club Consent Desk, a staff tool that reveals only whether a fictional participant may join a selected activity. Minimize stored metadata; protect mock staff sessions; redact background views and logs; validate deep links and hostile payloads; and prove expiry, denial, backgrounding, offline, malformed-link, and release-only behavior with layered tests. Transfer the design to a lost-device revocation. Real medical data, signatures, legal certification, and production identity stay out of scope.
