# Phase 6 — Motion, Gestures, Performance & Accessibility

**Days 29–34 · Outcome:** deliver responsive motion and high-scale UI with equivalent accessible interactions.

- Day 29: frame model, JS/UI threads, Reanimated shared values, worklets, timing, spring, interpolation, layout motion, and reduced motion.
- Day 30: gesture states, recognition, composition, pan/tap/long-press/pinch, velocity, snapping, cancellation, and alternatives.
- Day 31: frame budgets, release profiling, render causes, memoization cost, selector stability, startup, bundles, images, and memory.
- Day 32: measured virtualization, stable item rendering, pagination, image caching, prefetch, placeholders, and blank-area diagnosis.
- Day 33: VoiceOver, TalkBack, focus, roles, states, actions, contrast, large text, touch targets, modals, and announcements.
- Day 34: build the Choreography Cue Composer for a dance leader arranging hundreds of rehearsal cues. Provide interruptible reorder and duration gestures, deterministic snapping, undo, a virtualized cue sheet, stable rendering, and equivalent labelled controls for screen-reader, switch, keyboard, and reduced-motion users. Submit release-mode frame, render, startup, and memory traces, then transfer the interaction to locked cues. Audio, collaboration, cloud storage, and show-control hardware stay out of scope.
