# Phase 10 — Final Capstone: Lantern Live Festival Companion

**Days 53–57 · Outcome:** ship an inclusive, production-shaped festival companion and prove its offline, native, accessibility, security, performance, and recovery claims.

- Day 53: define attendee, accessibility-steward, and organizer decisions; measurable success; explicit non-goals; performance, venue, session, itinerary, announcement, memory, and sync models; typed routes; Redux/cache ownership; API and widget contracts; offline invariants; threats; permissions; operational budgets; tests; and ADRs.
- Day 54: build deep-linkable programme, performer, venue, and itinerary flows; an accessible venue map with optional foreground location and manual fallback; offline schedule caching; an idempotent itinerary queue; organizer-update conflicts; deterministic merge rules; freshness; cancellation; and sync-state UX.
- Day 55: add optional private camera memories with compression, metadata stripping, local-first ownership, and resumable mock upload; role-gated organizer announcements; schedule-change subscriptions; push actions; duplicate-safe deep links; quiet hours; foreground/background/killed-state handling; and a widget or Live Activity with explicit staleness.
- Day 56: prove unit, component, integration, E2E, native-extension, and failure-injection behavior; audit VoiceOver, TalkBack, dynamic type, reduced motion, contrast, focus, and mobility-aware venue tasks; profile programme lists, maps, images, gestures, startup, memory, and weak devices; and review organizer sessions, deep links, redaction, abuse, privacy, and dependencies.
- Day 57: create reproducible signed preview and production profiles; demonstrate a compatible update, phased rollout, and rollback; run offline-itinerary, schedule-conflict, denied-permission, and stale-widget scenarios; deliver architecture and threat diagrams, API/sync contracts, tests, traces, dashboards, runbooks, case study, limitations, and trade-off narrative.

Success is a five-scenario evidence pack: an offline festival weekend, denied permissions with useful fallbacks, a changed-session conflict that preserves attendee intent, a weak-device assistive-technology task, and recovery from a bad update. The transfer requirement closes a venue and reroutes affected plans without silently overwriting saved choices. Ticket sales, identity verification, a public social feed, real-time chat, continuous tracking, and a production event backend are explicitly out of scope.
