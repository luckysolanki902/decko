# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a learning project, and learners ask beginner questions —
that is the entire point of it existing.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Someone who misunderstood a lecture found a gap in the lecture.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This paragraph assumes closures" is useful; "did you even read it" is not.
- Take correction well. Being wrong in public is how a curriculum improves.
- Respect that contributors work at different paces and in different time zones, for free.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or derogatory comments about who someone is.
- Mocking anyone for what they do not know yet. There are no stupid questions here.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, code review, commit
messages and discussions — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the repository maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/luckysolanki902). Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge harmful. Enforcement
aims to be proportionate: most issues are resolved with a private word, and a first
mistake made in good faith is not treated as malice.
